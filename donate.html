What Your Contribution Helps Us Build

Maple Seeds is creating more than a place to meet. We are building a dependable community where children can learn together and families can form meaningful connections.

Educational Programs Hands-on classes, workshops, clubs, and enrichment opportunities for children of different ages.
Community Events Family gatherings, field trips, seasonal celebrations, and opportunities for parents and children to connect.
A Shared Home Base Supplies, furnishings, rent, and improvements that help make our collective space warm, useful, and welcoming.
A Community Library Books and shared educational resources that families can explore, enjoy, and learn from together.
